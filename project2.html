HRI for Disaster Management

Human-Robot Interaction Research Project

Project Overview

The aim of this project is to design an interactive system that supports emergency response teams in disaster environments using robotics and human-centered design principles. Our focus is on helping individuals who must remain indoors during wildfire smoke conditions.

Role

Researcher and UI/UX Designer

Technologies & Methods

  • User interviews and need analysis
  • Low and high fidelity prototyping
  • Human-Robot interaction design

Personal Reflection – Assignment 2 (Idil)

As someone living in Türkiye, wildfires are an increasingly familiar and concerning reality. Severe fires, especially in the southern regions, have caused both environmental and emotional damage. Observing how quickly smoke can endanger residential areas made me more aware of the challenges faced by people who cannot easily evacuate due to age or health issues. This personal connection motivated me to focus on solutions for individuals who must stay inside during wildfire conditions.

During the ideation phase, I developed and compared three primary solution concepts: a smart mask offering respiratory protection and health monitoring, a delivery robot providing essential items to users who cannot go outside, and a mobile AI application for real-time safety guidance and communication. Evaluating these options helped our team choose a direction grounded in feasibility and user needs.

I contributed significantly to the visualization and communication of our ideas. While preparing the presentation and storyboard, I actively used AI tools to generate clear and realistic images. This enhanced how effectively we could express each concept’s purpose and value to our audience and supported more informed feedback.

Working within a team made up of international and exchange students also broadened my perspective on accessibility and inclusivity in disaster scenarios. This experience strengthened my skills in user-centered thinking and design decision-making. In future work, involving users earlier through interviews and usability testing will make the outcomes even more human-focused.

Overall, I am satisfied with my contributions to shaping and presenting our solution concepts, and I believe this project helped me grow both creatively and strategically as a designer.
